# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive dashboard created using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze employee data, workforce distribution, and employee attrition.

The dashboard converts raw HR data into meaningful **KPIs, charts, and visual insights** that help understand employee demographics, attrition patterns, job satisfaction, salary slabs, departments, and work experience.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee workforce
- Track employee attrition and attrition rate
- Analyze attrition across departments
- Understand attrition based on salary slabs
- Analyze employee distribution by age group
- Compare attrition by gender
- Analyze job satisfaction across different job roles
- Understand attrition patterns based on work experience
- Provide clear and data-driven HR insights

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 🔹 Attrition by Department

Analyzes employee attrition across different departments:

- Administration
- Finance
- Human Resources
- IT
- Marketing
- Operations
- Sales

### 🔹 Attrition by Salary Slab

Analyzes employee attrition across different salary ranges:

- 0–3 LPA
- 3–6 LPA
- 6–10 LPA
- 10+ LPA

### 🔹 Attrition by Job Role & Job Satisfaction

Shows job satisfaction levels across different job roles and helps identify roles with higher or lower employee satisfaction.

### 🔹 Age Group Distribution

Shows employee distribution across different age groups:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

### 🔹 Attrition by Gender

Provides a comparison of employee attrition between male and female employees.

### 🔹 Attrition Trend by Experience

Analyzes employee attrition based on total years of work experience.

### 🔹 Department-wise Employee Count

Shows the number of employees working in each department and helps compare workforce distribution.
